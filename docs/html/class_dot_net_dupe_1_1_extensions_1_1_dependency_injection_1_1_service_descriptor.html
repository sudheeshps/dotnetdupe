Detailed Description

Describes a service with its service type, implementation, and lifetime.

Modeled after Microsoft.Extensions.DependencyInjection.ServiceDescriptor. Encapsulates metadata required by a ServiceProvider to resolve dependencies, supporting instance registrations, factory delegates, and type-based instantiation. [Microsoft.Extensions.DependencyInjection]
